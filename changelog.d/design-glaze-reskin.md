### Changed

- New look based on the Glaze design system. The dark theme is pure black, the light theme is pure white, the accent is cobalt, and corners are rounder.
- Figtree is now the font of the app, the web client and the status page.
- Larger, easier-to-read text, and a centered chat column on wide windows.
- Main buttons, the send button and the focus ring are cobalt. The message box is rounder and stands up from the page.
- The sidebar is cleaner: bolder agent names, no role chip beside each name, and a taller search field. The open page in the side rail and in settings is marked in cobalt.
- Settings sections are white cards with a thin border (dark cards in the dark theme) instead of grey blocks.
- New icons throughout the app, from the Hugeicons set that Glaze uses, with a lighter line.
- Icons move, as in the Glaze animated icons: an icon plays a short animation when you point at its button and when its page opens. The animation is off when the system asks for reduced motion.
- The app is named Sumit, with a new cat logo and app icon on desktop and mobile. The name is one setting (`APP_NAME`), and a rename keeps the agents, chats and sign-in.
- Agents are cat and panda mascots in the Duolingo manner. The avatar's shape picks the animal and its markings, its expression picks the face, and its colour is the fur or the backdrop. Saved avatars stay the same.
- Talk to make agents: in live voice, a request for new work makes a new agent, named after the work, that starts on it at once and speaks its result. Follow-ups go to that agent. Live voice is on by default.
- An agent that live voice makes gets its name, description and mascot from the profile AI, as in the new-agent form.
- A Talk button in the side rail opens the voice conversation of the open chat in one click.
