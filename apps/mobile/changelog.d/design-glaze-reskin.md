### Changed

- New look that matches the desktop app: a pure black dark theme, a pure white light theme, a cobalt accent and main buttons, the Figtree font, and new icons from the Hugeicons set.
- The app is named Sumit, with the cat logo, and agents are cat and panda mascots, as on desktop.
- Talk: tap the Talk button on the home screen and say a task. A new agent named after the task starts on it, its chat opens, and the phone reads the answer aloud and listens for your next request.
- Talk asks the AI on your computer or server to name the new agent and choose its description and mascot. If it does not answer in time, the first words of the task name it.
